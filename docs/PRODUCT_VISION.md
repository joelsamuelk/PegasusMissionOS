# Product Vision

## Company vision
Pegasus builds the operating system for mission-driven organisations.

## Brand promise
Every mission deserves world-class technology.

## Positioning
Pegasus Mission OS gives mission-driven organisations one intelligent place to
discover funding, manage grant applications, run funded programmes, track
outcomes, demonstrate impact and manage the operational work surrounding their
mission. Over time it becomes the system through which an organisation
understands and runs its entire operation.

It is deliberately **not** any one of these things alone:

- a charity CRM
- a generic project management tool
- a grant database
- an AI writing assistant
- a reporting dashboard

It is an integrated organisational operating system built specifically for
mission-driven work.

## Initial target customer
A UK-based charity, NGO, community interest company, foundation or social
enterprise with 3 to 100 team members, limited operations capacity, multiple
active funding applications, funding records in spreadsheets, evidence spread
across documents, significant manual reporting work, no dedicated data or
technology team, and a need to demonstrate credible impact to funders and
trustees.

The demonstration workspace, Northstar Community Foundation, is exactly this
kind of organisation: it supports young people aged 14 to 25 through employment
readiness, digital skills, mentoring and mental wellbeing support.

## Working principles
1. Build a focused, credible MVP rather than a shallow collection of features.
2. Prioritise clarity, trust, accessibility, data security and excellent
   workflows.
3. Use AI to reduce administrative work, not to make unsupported decisions.
4. Every AI-generated output must be editable, explainable and reviewable.
5. Never fabricate funding requirements, organisation data, outcomes or impact
   evidence.
6. Clearly distinguish verified, organisation-provided, inferred and
   AI-generated information.
7. Design for small organisations first, with an architecture that can support
   large international NGOs later.
8. UK English throughout, no em dashes in interface copy.
9. The product must feel premium, calm, intelligent and highly intentional.

## Tone
Calm, direct UK English. We say "Review funding match", "Add supporting
evidence", "Three application answers need review". We never say "Supercharge
your impact", "Unlock game-changing AI" or "Revolutionise your charity".
