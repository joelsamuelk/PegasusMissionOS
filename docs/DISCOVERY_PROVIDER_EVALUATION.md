# Discovery provider evaluation

Evaluated 19 August 2026. Provider-specific records remain integration metadata; no core commercial entity depends on any provider.

| Provider                                 | Coverage and strengths                                                                                                                             | Constraints                                                                                                                                   | Decision                                                                                                                                               |
| ---------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Charity Commission for England and Wales | Authoritative, live charity identity and register data; official filings and daily full-register extracts; strong Mission OS provenance            | Beta API, authenticated key and rate limits; England and Wales rather than all UK; limited change/news and professional-role coverage         | **Selected for Mission OS identity and registry research.** Use the API for small live runs and the official daily extract for future batch discovery. |
| Companies House                          | Authoritative, live UK company identity; good API and 600 requests per five minutes; strong registration provenance                                | Company registry rather than commercial-intent source; weak charity, people and event coverage                                                | **Selected for Studio identity corroboration**, not as the primary change-signal finder.                                                               |
| Brave Search API                         | Broad public-web coverage for announcements, careers, reports and change signals; explicit API, rate-limit headers and predictable query interface | Search results are discovery evidence, not verified facts; requires server key and careful official-source filtering; pricing depends on plan | **Selected public-web search adapter.** Results must lead to first-party research before claims are trusted.                                           |
| 360Giving                                | Authoritative open UK grant data and useful funding provenance                                                                                     | Grant coverage depends on publisher participation; not a full organisation or people directory                                                | Candidate Mission OS funding-signal adapter after the pilot.                                                                                           |
| Commercial people/company databases      | Potential structured people and firmographic breadth                                                                                               | Variable UK charity coverage, cost, provenance and personal-data/terms considerations                                                         | Deferred until measured gaps justify a vendor and a compliance review.                                                                                 |

## Pilot architecture

The first live path uses Brave Search to find small sets of event-led candidates and official pages, Charity Commission data for England/Wales charity identity, and Companies House for UK company identity. Official organisation pages and documents are researched through a bounded fetcher: five pages, two external sources, 60,000 total source characters, 15 seconds, and no automatic scheduling. Credentials are server-only. Missing credentials produce `not_configured`; successful health checks produce `connected`; failed checks produce `configured_unhealthy`.

Primary documentation: [Charity Commission API](https://register-of-charities.charitycommission.gov.uk/en/documentation-on-the-api), [Charity Commission full-register download](https://register-of-charities.charitycommission.gov.uk/en/register/full-register-download), [Companies House API](https://developer.company-information.service.gov.uk/), [Companies House developer limits](https://developer.company-information.service.gov.uk/developer-guidelines), and [Brave Search rate limits](https://api-dashboard.search.brave.com/documentation/guides/rate-limiting).
